Plumbing questions, answered

The things Melbourne homeowners and property managers ask us most.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing in Melbourne?

Yes. Our emergency plumbers are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Melbourne's east and south-east. Call 1300 000 000 and we dispatch the closest licensed plumber — usually inside a 60-minute window for urgent jobs.

How much does a plumber cost in Melbourne?

Standard weekday call-outs start from $99 plus parts, and we confirm a fixed price in writing before any work begins. Out-of-hours and emergency rates are quoted upfront so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Which Melbourne suburbs do you service?

We cover Bayswater, Boronia, Ringwood, Wantirna, Knoxfield, Ferntree Gully, Rowville, Glen Waverley, Mount Waverley, Croydon, Mitcham, Vermont, Scoresby, Wheelers Hill, Doncaster, Balwyn and surrounding suburbs. See the full coverage list.

Are you licensed and insured?

Every plumber on our team holds a current Victorian plumbing licence, and we carry full public liability and workers compensation insurance. Licence and insurance details are included with every quote.

What is backflow testing and how often is it required?

Backflow testing checks that a prevention device is stopping contaminated water from flowing back into the mains supply. Melbourne properties with a containment or zone device generally need it tested every 12 months by a licensed tester, with results lodged to the local water authority. Learn more about backflow prevention testing.

Can you help with a blocked drain that keeps coming back?

Yes. Recurring blockages usually mean tree roots, a collapsed pipe or heavy grease build-up. We run a CCTV drain camera to find the exact cause, then recommend jet cleaning, pipe relining or excavation based on what we actually see.
