Good to know

Plumbing questions Melbourne asks us most

Do you charge a call-out fee?
No. Alltype Plumbing quotes before we start and there's no call-out fee for standard metro Melbourne suburbs during business hours. You approve the fixed price first, so the invoice matches the quote.
How fast can a plumber get to me in an emergency?
For burst pipes, sewage overflows and no-water emergencies we aim to be on site within 60 minutes across inner Melbourne, and same-day for outer suburbs. Call 0400 981 494 and tell us your suburb — we'll give you an honest ETA, not a vague "sometime today".
What is backflow testing and do I legally need it?
Backflow prevention devices stop contaminated water siphoning back into the mains supply. Victorian regulations require devices at many commercial, industrial and multi-dwelling properties to be tested annually by an accredited tester, with results lodged with your water authority. See our backflow testing service.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured?
Yes. Every plumber holds a current Victorian Plumbing Industry Commission licence and works under full public liability and WorkCover insurance. Licence and insurance details are supplied with your quote.
Which Melbourne suburbs do you cover?
Our core patch is 16 suburbs — Richmond, Fitzroy, Brunswick, Carlton, South Yarra, Hawthorn, Preston, Coburg, Footscray, Docklands, St Kilda, Essendon, Moonee Ponds, Glen Waverley, Ringwood and Werribee — plus surrounding areas on request. Not sure? Just call.
Can you fix a blocked drain without digging up my garden?
Usually yes. We run a CCTV camera down the line to find the exact cause, then clear it with high-pressure jetting or an electric eel. Where the pipe itself is cracked or root-invaded, trenchless pipe relining is available and we'll walk you through the cost difference.
