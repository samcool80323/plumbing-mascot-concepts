Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what Melbourne homeowners and property managers ask Pipe Pete most.

How much does an emergency plumber cost in Melbourne? +
Standard call-outs start with a fixed attendance and diagnosis fee, quoted before we leave the depot, plus the cost of the repair. After-hours and weekend jobs carry a higher rate. You'll always get the price in writing before work starts — no open-ended hourly surprises.
How quickly can you get to me? +
For urgent jobs inside our core suburbs we aim to be on site within about 60 minutes, traffic permitting. If we can't make that window we'll tell you honestly on the phone and give you a realistic time instead of guessing.
What does backflow testing involve? +
A licensed plumber tests your backflow prevention device against the AS/NZS 2845 standard, records the results, and issues a compliance certificate for your water authority. Most commercial properties need it annually, and we can set up reminders so it never lapses.
Do you fix blocked drains or just clear them? +
Both. We clear the blockage with jetting or an electric eel, then run a CCTV camera through the line to identify tree roots, cracked earthenware or fat build-up so the problem doesn't come back next winter. Repair options are quoted separately and only if you want them.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes — all plumbing and gasfitting work is carried out by licensed tradespeople, and the business holds public liability cover. Licence and insurance details are available on request and printed on every invoice.
Do you work with property managers and body corporates? +
Absolutely. We handle multi-site maintenance, common-property drainage, annual backflow compliance and after-hours tenant emergencies, with one point of contact and consolidated reporting.
